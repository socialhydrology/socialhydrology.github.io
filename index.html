About Our Group

Welcome to the Socio-Hydrology Research Group. Our interdisciplinary team investigates the two-way feedbacks between human societies and hydrological processes, combining hydrological modeling, remote sensing, and social science to address water security and climate adaptation challenges.

Research Themes

Coupled Human–Water Modeling

Simulating dynamic interactions between water policy, land-use change, and river basin hydrology.

Flood Risk & Delta Resilience

Assessing community adaptation, polder management, and flood vulnerability in deltaic regions.

Water Governance & Equity

Analyzing institutional frameworks, groundwater depletion, and equitable access to water resources.
